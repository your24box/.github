# 行为准则 · Code of Conduct

福州友宝电子科技有限公司（Fuzhou Youbao Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.）以专业、克制、可审计的方式维护本 GitHub 组织。本组织面向客户展示工程代码，不是开放协作社区。

This GitHub organization is maintained by Fuzhou Youbao Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. It is a customer-facing engineering showcase, not an open-contribution community.

## 中文

### 我们期望

- 讨论聚焦产品、接口、质量与交付，保持礼貌与事实依据。
- 尊重客户、合作伙伴与同事的时间，不发布无关营销或广告。
- 未经授权，不披露客户项目、合同、数据、密钥或未公开仓库内容。
- 不提交恶意代码、破坏性测试、爬虫或未授权访问尝试。
- Issue 与讨论中不包含真实用户个人信息、运单号或可识别现场信息。

### 不可接受的行为

- 骚扰、歧视、人身攻击或侮辱性言论。
- 泄露客户机密、内部凭证或生产配置。
- 将本组织仓库用于与邮宝业务无关的用途。
- 在公开渠道讨论未授权的安全漏洞细节（请按 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 私下报告）。

### 执行

违反本准则的内容或账号，我们可能关闭讨论、删除内容或限制访问，并在必要时通知相关客户或法律顾问。

报告问题： [suncai@24box.cn](mailto:suncai@24box.cn) 或致电 0591-83638765。

## English

### We expect

- Keep discussion focused on product, interfaces, quality, and delivery. Be courteous and factual.
- Respect the time of clients, partners, and colleagues. Do not post unrelated marketing or advertising.
- Do not disclose customer projects, contracts, data, secrets, or unpublished repository contents without authorization.
- Do not submit malware, destructive tests, scraping, or unauthorized access attempts.
- Do not include real personal data, tracking numbers, or identifiable site details in public issues or discussions.

### Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or abusive language.
- Leaking customer confidential information, internal credentials, or production configuration.
- Using organization repositories for purposes unrelated to Youbao business.
- Discussing unauthorized security vulnerability details in public channels (report privately via [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

### Enforcement

We may close discussions, remove content, or restrict access for violations, and notify relevant clients or legal counsel when required.

Report concerns: [suncai@24box.cn](mailto:suncai@24box.cn) or +86 591 8363 8765.
