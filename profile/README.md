### 关于我们

福州友宝电子科技有限公司成立于 2012 年 9 月，总部设于福州，2014 年在杭州设立研发中心。公司专注物联网技术的开发与应用，主营智能快递终端系统的研发、销售，以及基于末端网点的社区便民服务。

核心产品「邮宝」智能快件箱是联网储物柜：快递员将快件存入指定柜机后，系统自动向收件人发送取件地址与凭证；收件人可在方便时到柜取件，无需当面交接。这一模式帮助快递公司提高末端派送效率，帮助物业提升服务水平，并保护用户收件隐私。

This GitHub organization is the customer-facing code showcase of Fuzhou Youbao Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. Private repositories for contracted projects are shared under separate access agreements.

### About us

Founded in September 2012 and headquartered in Fuzhou, with an R&D center established in Hangzhou in 2014, we specialize in IoT research and application. Our core business is the development and sale of intelligent express terminal systems, together with community convenience services built on last-mile sites.

Youbao (24box) is a networked parcel locker. After a courier deposits a shipment, the system notifies the recipient with the pickup location and credential. Recipients collect parcels when it suits them—no in-person handoff required. The model improves last-mile efficiency for carriers, service quality for property managers, and privacy for end users.
